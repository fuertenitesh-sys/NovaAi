Find the answer.
See where it came from.

Connect approved documents and business data so your team can ask questions and get answers with sources. Keep missing or conflicting information visible for review.

EXAMPLE PROJECT

A renewal needs review.

Before renewing a contract, your team needs its terms, dates, and risks in one place. AI prepares a summary linked to the original documents for a person to check.

01

INFORMATION AI USES

Connect.

  • Contract and account records
  • Approved policies and guidance
  • Relevant source documents
02

WHAT AI PREPARES

Understand.

  • Find the relevant documents
  • Highlight terms and conflicting details
  • Link each answer to its source
03

WHAT YOUR TEAM DOES

Decide.

  • Check the supporting evidence
  • Check missing or unclear information
  • Make the final decision

INFORMATION AI USES

02

WHAT AI PREPARES

Prepare.

  • Prepare an update or route the case

WHAT YOUR TEAM DOES

  • Decide what needs attention first

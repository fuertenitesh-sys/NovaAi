Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

Nova.ai is designed around zero-data retention, edge-isolated audio inference, and cryptographic enterprise boundary enforcement.

1. Data Minimization & Zero Audio Retention

Voice interactions processed through Nova's sub-200ms Direct S2S WebRTC pipeline operate with ephemeral memory buffers. Unless an enterprise customer explicitly enables compliance recording inside their private VPC, raw voice audio is processed in-memory and immediately purged at the conclusion of each conversational turn.

2. Enterprise VPC & On-Premises Isolation

For regulated institutions, Nova delivers dedicated single-tenant VPC deployments on AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure, as well as on-premises edge appliances. Your conversational data never traverses shared public LLM endpoints or trains foundational public models.

3. Encryption Standards

  • In Transit: WebRTC DTLS/SRTP 256-bit AES encryption for all live audio streams; TLS 1.3 for API endpoints.
  • At Rest: Customer-managed encryption keys (KMS / HSM) with AES-256 for CRM metadata and transcripts.
  • Biometrics: Irreversible acoustic voiceprint vector hashes; no reconstructable raw voice recordings stored.

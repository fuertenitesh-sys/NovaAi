BEFORE PEOPLE DEPEND ON IT

Ready for
real work.

These questions shape the system from the beginning.

Where will the AI run?

We review your data policies, existing servers, cloud setup, and budget. Then we agree where the AI will run and explain any external services it needs.

What can the system see and do?

We agree which documents and systems the AI can access, what it can change, and which actions need a person’s approval.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

We test difficult cases and errors before launch. We decide when the AI should stop, ask a person for help, or use a backup process, and record issues so they can be investigated.

How will people learn to use it?

Your people try the system during development. We train them on real tasks, explain how to check results, and use their feedback to improve it.

How are timing and pricing set?

Your proposal sets out what we will build, the systems to connect, the team, delivery dates, fees, and ongoing support. We agree these details before work begins.

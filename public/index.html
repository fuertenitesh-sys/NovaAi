What does “sub-200ms voice intelligence” mean in practice?

Traditional voice bots take 1.5 to 2 seconds to convert speech to text, generate a response, and convert back to speech. Nova streams neural audio tokens directly, achieving natural <200ms human turn-taking, allowing smooth mid-sentence interruptions without awkward pauses.

What does “your in-house AI team” actually mean?

You work with a dedicated team of forward-deployed engineers from Nova without recruiting an internal AI department. Our engineers build and connect the systems, train your users, and provide ongoing support.

How does Nova integrate with our existing software and CRM?

Nova connects natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, PostgreSQL, internal REST/GraphQL APIs, and enterprise calendar systems. Calls and customer actions update your records in real time.

Is customer voice data secure and HIPAA / SOC-2 compliant?

Yes. All voice streams are end-to-end encrypted with zero data retention for unauthorized training. We support private VPC and on-premise deployments for healthcare and financial institutions.

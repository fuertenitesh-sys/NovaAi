BEFORE WE BUILD

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Ask us something else
What does “your in-house AI team” actually mean?

You work with a dedicated team from Nova, without recruiting a new AI department. Our engineers build and connect the systems, train your users, and provide ongoing support. They remain Nova engineers; the team, responsibilities, and support are agreed before work begins.

When do we need Nova instead of a ready-made AI tool?

A ready-made tool may be enough for individual tasks. Nova helps when AI needs to use your business data, connect several systems, follow your approval rules, or become part of how a whole team works. We agree what needs building and which existing tools to use.

Can you work with our existing technology team?

Yes. We work with your existing engineering, IT, security, and business teams. They set the requirements and standards; our engineers build and deploy the AI with them.

Can AI run in our own environment?

Yes. We can design for your private cloud, your own servers, or a combination of both. We review your data policies, infrastructure, AI requirements, and budget first, and explain any external services the system needs.

How do you decide whether it is working?

We record how the work performs today and agree on a target with you. That could mean faster customer responses, better sales follow-up, fewer errors, or less manual work. After launch, we check the results and whether your team is using the system.

How are timing and pricing agreed?

We first agree on the problem, the systems to connect, and the support you need. Your proposal sets out the team, delivery stages, timing, and price. Further work is planned with you as your needs grow.
